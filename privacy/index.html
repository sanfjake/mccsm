Website Information and Contact

This website provides general information about Mini-Cassia Chiropractic & Sports Medicine and links that allow visitors to call or text the clinic.

The new website prototype does not contain an online patient intake form or booking portal. Following a text or call link opens a separate application provided by your device or carrier; those communication systems have their own privacy and security characteristics.

Medical details and ordinary messages

Please do not send sensitive medical details through ordinary SMS text, email, or voicemail. If you need to discuss health information, contact the office to arrange an appropriate communication method.

Website hosting and external services

The site uses static web hosting. Like many web services, hosting providers may process technical connection information such as IP addresses and request logs. Links to external mapping services may lead to websites with separate privacy policies.

Questions

To ask general questions about the website, contact the clinic by calling or texting (208) 260-0917. Do not use this website page to submit medical records.
