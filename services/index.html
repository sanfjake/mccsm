CARE OPTIONS

Services at Our Rupert Clinic

Care begins with an evaluation and discussion of your goals. These are the services currently described by our clinic; specific care options depend on individual assessment.

Chiropractic Adjustments

Chiropractic care focused on joint movement, function, and your individual goals. We'll discuss how an adjustment may fit your care plan.

Sports-Focused Care

Evaluation and treatment planning for active people with movement- or activity-related concerns, including those who want to return to their regular routines.

Rehabilitative Care

Exercises and movement strategies selected for your needs and activities. Your plan may include techniques you can practice between visits.

Dry Needling

Dry needling may be an option for appropriate patients following evaluation and discussion of potential benefits and risks.

Soft-Tissue Care

Hands-on soft-tissue techniques may be included as part of an individualized approach to care.

Common Questions

Do I need to choose a treatment before contacting you?

No. You can arrange an appointment to discuss your concerns and care options. The most appropriate approach depends on your individual circumstances.

Is a private treatment room available?

Yes. Our clinic has an open-concept treatment area, and a separate private room is available when additional privacy is needed. Ask about it when arranging your visit.

Can I ask about pricing or availability by text?

Yes, you can text the office with general scheduling or administrative questions. Please avoid putting detailed medical information in an ordinary text message.

Have a question? Get in touch.

Texting is the best way to reach Dr. Sanford for scheduling and general questions. If you call while he's with a patient, please leave a voicemail. A text message does not confirm an appointment.
