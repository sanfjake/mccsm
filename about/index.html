A LOCAL PRACTICE

Meet Dr. Jake Sanford, DC

Mini-Cassia Chiropractic & Sports Medicine is a locally owned practice in Rupert, Idaho. I personally run the office, from meeting patients to returning calls and messages.

My approach begins by listening to your goals, talking through the concerns that brought you in, and explaining care options. I want you to feel comfortable asking questions and understanding what to expect.

Because I work directly with patients throughout the day, I may not be able to answer every call. Texting is the easiest way to reach me for general questions and scheduling. If you prefer to call, please leave a voicemail if I don't answer.
